## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for higher education faculty, department chairs, and curriculum audit committees. It transforms bureaucratic syllabus design and accreditation reporting into a deliberate, scholarly, and frictionless editing experience.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Scholarly Authority:** Projects intellectual rigor and administrative precision through structured typographic hierarchies and understated, purposeful interfaces.
- **Editorial Calm:** Evokes the peaceful focus of an academic reading room by utilizing warm parchment canvases, avoiding aggressive saturations, dark UI traps, or visual noise.
- **Institutional Confidence:** Delivers unambiguous accreditation validation signals (Course Outcomes, Program Outcomes, Bloom's taxonomy verification) through clear, restrained status indicators.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design language draws from **Modern Editorial Document Ergonomics** coupled with structured utilitarian enterprise architecture. It pairs warm archival paper tones with sharp, micro-bordered white working panels, tabular density, and refined editorial typography. There are no decorative illustrations, novelty gradients, or simulated dimensionality; hierarchy is established entirely through structural borders, typographic nuance, and functional tinting.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic foundations with institutional burgundy accents and rigorous operational tokens.

### Base Canvases & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (warm ivory parchment) provides a non-glare, comfortable background for prolonged reading and audit sessions.
- **App Shell & Sub-canvases:** `#F5F2EB` (soft parchment) defines secondary sidebars and workflow trays.
- **Work Surfaces:** Pure `#FFFFFF` anchors active specification cards, matrix tables, and editor sheets.
- **Structural Dividers:** `#E7E3DC` forms subtle hairline containment borders between panels, table cells, and header regions.

### Brand & Interactive Tiers
- **Primary Brand (Deep Burgundy):** `#781D32` anchors core calls-to-action, navigation selection states, and formal document headers.
- **Hover Primary:** `#611527` for pressed and hover states on primary actions.
- **Secondary Accent:** `#8E233C` provides contrast for subheadings, active tab highlights, and badge accents.
- **Soft Burgundy Tint:** `#FAF0F2` acts as the subtle background for selected workflow items, active row mappings, and input focus rings.

### Typography & Content Neutrals
- **Primary Text:** `#1E232A` (deep slate-charcoal) guarantees AAA contrast for body paragraphs, audit text, and table figures.
- **Secondary Body:** `#374151` for labels, descriptions, and supporting field copy.
- **Muted Meta:** `#6B7280` strictly reserved for timestamps, disabled states, and auxiliary hints.

### Functional Accreditation & Audit Tiers
- **Valid / OK / Met:** `#15803D` text on `#F0FDF4` fill with `#BBF7D0` border.
- **Warning / Advisory:** `#B45309` text on `#FFFBEB` fill with `#FDE68A` border.
- **Error / Deficient:** `#B91C1C` text on `#FEF2F2` fill with `#FECACA` border.
- **Info / Pending Review:** `#1D4ED8` text on `#EFF6FF` fill with `#BFDBFE` border.

## Typography

The typographic system relies on **Inter** with precise optical sizing, neutral mechanics, and high tabular legibility. For code-like identifiers (course codes like `CS401`, outcome weights, and formula calculations), **JetBrains Mono** provides fixed-width structural clarity.

### Editorial Hierarchies
- **Headlines:** Use tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and measured weights (`600` and `700`) to evoke book jacket typography without feeling display-heavy or decorative.
- **Body Text:** Scaled at `0.875rem` (`14px`) for optimal information density within dense forms, curriculum guidelines, and accreditation rubrics. Line height is set to `1.45rem` to prevent visual fatigue during lengthy editorial sessions.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Rendered in semi-bold uppercase or title case with slight positive letter spacing (`0.025em` to `0.04em`) to establish crisp section boundaries.
- **Tabular Figures:** All financial, credit-hour, and CO-PO alignment matrices must employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure exact vertical scanning across columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured editorial workbench composed of a top utility header, a persistent left workflow pane, and an expansive central document stage.

### Application Shell Structure
- **Global Top Bar:** Fixed `56px` height. Houses the academic wordmark, current course identifier badge, role switcher pills (Author, Reviewer, HOD), and global status chips (e.g., "Review Pending").
- **Workflow Navigation (Left Sidebar):** Width locked at `260px` on desktop. Houses linear progress steps: `Upload`, `Spec`, `CO-PO-PSO map`, `Checks`, `Workflow`, and `Export`.
- **Working Canvas (Central Area):** Employs a max-width container (`1440px`) centered on screens over `1600px`, preserving optimal line length while permitting multi-column matrix tables to expand.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** Full dual-pane interface. Active canvas displays multi-column mapping matrices (e.g., 6 Course Outcomes mapped against 12 Program Outcomes) with sticky row and column headers.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Left workflow sidebar collapses into an icon-and-step drawer; margins reduce from `2rem` to `1.5rem`; matrix views switch to horizontally scrolling data cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Navigation collapses to a top off-canvas sheet; course audits reflow into stacked verification cards; complex matrix cells convert into accordion accordances.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately minimizes simulated elevation. Depth is achieved via **tonal planar containment** and **subtle hairline bounding lines**.

### Architectural Elevation Rules
- **Flat Surface Tiering:** 
  - `Level 0 (Base Canvas):` Warm ivory (`#FAF8F5`).
  - `Level 1 (Navigation & Auxiliary Bars):` Soft parchment (`#F5F2EB`) bounded by `#E7E3DC` borders.
  - `Level 2 (Active Cards & Document Canvas):` Crisp `#FFFFFF` with `#E7E3DC` borders.
- **Shadow Profiles:**
  - **Resting Panes & Tables:** Strictly no drop shadow. Separation is achieved through `1px solid #E7E3DC`.
  - **Dropdowns & Popovers:** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04)` with a `1px solid #E7E3DC` perimeter.
  - **Modals & Audit Sheets:** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.10), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Focus & Interaction Cues:** Instead of luminous glows, interactive focus states leverage a clean `2px` ring in deep burgundy (`#781D32`) offset by `2px` white space.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a disciplined **Soft (`1`)** shape language that reflects traditional stationery, binder registers, and formal institutional documents.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Data Cells:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius. Evokes precision tool ergonomics without harsh pointed corners.
- **Cards, Audit Panels, and Table Shells:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) outer radius with nested elements using `4px`.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Standard tags and workflow badges utilize `4px` radius. Role Switchers (Author / Reviewer / HOD) utilize rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish user state toggles from document content.

## Components

- **Primary Action (e.g., "Submit for Review", "Export PDF"):** Background `#781D32`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `transparent`. Hover `#611527`. Active `#4D0E1D`. Padding: `0.5rem 1rem` (`8px 16px`). Font: `label-lg`. Radius: `4px`.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Save Draft", "Add Outcome"):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E232A`, border `1px solid #E7E3DC`. Hover `#F5F2EB`. Active `#EDE8DF`.
- **Ghost Action:** Background transparent, text `#781D32`. Hover background `#FAF0F2`.

### Role Switcher Pills
- **Container:** Segmented control track in `#F5F2EB` with `1px solid #E7E3DC` border and `9999px` radius.
- **Inactive Tab:** Text `#6B7280`, transparent background.
- **Active Role Tab:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#781D32`, subtle `1px solid #E7E3DC` border, weight `600`, pill radius.

### Document Status Chips & Audit Badges
- **Review Pending (Info):** Background `#EFF6FF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`, text `#1D4ED8`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
- **Accredited / Met (Valid):** Background `#F0FDF4`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`, text `#15803D`.
- **Advisory (Warning):** Background `#FFFBEB`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, text `#B45309`.
- **Deficient (Error):** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FECACA`, text `#B91C1C`.
- Badges feature a preceding `6px` solid status dot for immediate optical recognition.

### Input Fields & Structured Form Controls
- **Text & Textarea Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E3DC`, text `#1E232A`, placeholder `#9CA3AF`. Padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`. Focus ring `2px solid #781D32` with `0` blur.
- **Course Description & Syllabus Inputs:** Rendered on clean white containers framed by hairline borders, providing ample vertical breathing room.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Checkbox:** Square `16px x 16px` box with `3px` radius. Border `1.5px solid #D1D5DB`. Checked state: background `#781D32`, white check icon.
- **Radio Button:** `16px x 16px` circle. Active state: inner filled circle `#781D32` with white gutter.

### CO-PO-PSO Alignment Matrix
- **Header Cells:** Background `#F5F2EB`, border `1px solid #E7E3DC`, uppercase `label-md` tracking.
- **Data Matrix Cells:** Background `#FFFFFF`, hover `#FAF0F2`. Level scores (`1 - Slight`, `2 - Moderate`, `3 - Substantial`) selectable via compact dropdown or segment toggle with monospace indicator styling.
- **Correlation Totals:** Sticky bottom summary row highlighted in `#FAF8F5` with bold `code-tabular` figures.

### Workflow Navigation Stepper
- **Vertical Step List:** Connected via vertical `1.5px` hairline divider in `#E7E3DC`.
- **Step States:**
  - *Completed:* `#15803D` check icon in soft green disc.
  - *Current / Active:* `#781D32` solid numbered badge with `#FAF0F2` highlight card container.
  - *Upcoming:* `#9CA3AF` hollow border with muted text.
