4. Course Contents (Units, Topics & Hours)

auto_awesome edited by AI
Unit 1: Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrences
6 Lec + 2 Tut 8 hrs

Growth of functions; Big-O, Omega, Theta notation; Master Theorem (all 3 cases and generalized form); Akra-Bazzi method overview; Amortized analysis: Aggregate, Accounting, and Potential methods.

edited by AI Reordered Amortized Analysis into Unit 1 for pedagogical flow.
Unit 2: Advanced Linear Structures & Priority Queues
7 Lec + 2 Tut 9 hrs

Dynamic arrays & resizing strategies; Skip Lists with randomized analysis; Binary Heaps, d-ary Heaps; Binomial & Fibonacci Heaps with amortized decrease-key proofs; Disjoint-Set Union-Find with path compression.

Unit 3: Balanced Search Trees & Multiway Trees
8 Lec + 3 Tut 11 hrs

Binary Search Tree invariants; AVL Trees (rotations, balance factors); Red-Black Trees (insertion & deletion invariant recovery); Splay Trees; Multiway search: 2-3-4 Trees, B-Trees & B+ Trees for external memory indices.

Unit 4: Graph Algorithms & Network Flows
9 Lec + 3 Tut 12 hrs

Graph representations; BFS, DFS, Strongly Connected Components (Tarjan & Kosaraju); Minimum Spanning Trees (Kruskal, Prim); Single-source & All-pairs shortest paths (Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall); Ford-Fulkerson & Edmonds-Karp Max-Flow.

Unit 5: Intractability & NP-Completeness
12 Lec + 4 Tut 16 hrs

Tractable vs Intractable problems; Class P, NP, NP-Hard, NP-Complete; Polynomial-time verification; Cook-Levin theorem implications; Canonical reductions: 3-SAT to CLIQUE, VERTEX-COVER, SUBSET-SUM; Approximation algorithms introductory concepts.
